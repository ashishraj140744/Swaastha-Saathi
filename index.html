How Swaastha Saathi works

1. You describe symptoms

In plain English or Hindi — no medical jargon needed.

2. AI-assisted triage

We match your symptoms to a likely category and urgency using a transparent, rule-based system — every suggestion traces back to what you said.

3. We route you, not diagnose you

You're connected to the nearest suitable facility, NGO camp, or doctor. A qualified doctor always makes the final call.

4. ASHA workers get smart prioritization

Household visit order is ranked by an explainable scoring formula — risk factors, time since last visit, and pending health items.
